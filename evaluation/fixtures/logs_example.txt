Service reliability examples — locally authored operating model

Severity is assigned from observed effect, breadth, integrity exposure and time to recovery.
The categories below are policy anchors for the demo, not claims about any specific deployment.

Priority | Operational condition | Example evidence
P0 | A customer-facing entry point has no ready replicas | All API replicas refused readiness probes
P0 | Durable state cannot be trusted or two writers can diverge | Ledger verification found a broken hash chain
P0 | A required write majority or signing trust root is lost | Commit quorum lost; writes are being rejected
P1 | A recovery mechanism or a key service objective is materially impaired | Replica apply lag exceeded 90 seconds
P1 | A regional request path rejects a sustained share of valid traffic | Rejected requests 44% at regional checkout
P2 | A bounded workflow misses its delivery or response objective | Scheduled export missed its delivery window
P2 | One shard or data view is degraded while other paths remain usable | Search index is serving an older generation
P3 | A measured indicator approaches a locally declared operating guardrail | Queue depth reached 72 percent of its guardrail
P4 | A change or observation is useful context without a demonstrated fault | Canary rollout reached its observation window
P5 | Routine instrumentation records normal control-loop activity | Lease extension acknowledged for shard cobalt

Routing follows the named owning component when known. Unrecognized component labels default to
application ownership. Security-sensitive identity and signing evidence routes to security.
Severity phrases are intentionally narrow: ambiguous text remains P4 for human review. Numerical
limits in these examples are demonstration thresholds and should be replaced by service objectives
before operational use.
